Eozin

Eozin is the metallic lustre of Zsolnay ceramics, introduced in Pécs in 1893. In a smoky reduction firing, copper and silver ions migrate into the glaze and precipitate as metal nanoparticles. That film, a few hundred nanometres deep, colours light by plasmon absorption and thin-film interference.
